Program Guidelines

KEEP THE OFFER
CLEAR & APPROVED.

Do

  • Present the incentive as a complimentary thank-you or promotional benefit.
  • Use current DreamKeys materials and approved talking points.
  • Explain that hotel accommodations are included and applicable taxes and required travel fees remain the recipient's responsibility.
  • Provide current terms and conditions.

Don't

  • Do not describe it as a “free trip,” “completely free,” or “no fees.”
  • Do not promise a specific hotel, date, upgrade, availability or exact value unless current terms provide it.
  • Do not quote outdated fees or alter the terms.
  • Do not promise transportation or other travel components unless specifically included in the applicable incentive.
